01 — 非雨Company

RealRain · realrain.co

让想象世界，
进入现实生活。 Imagined worlds, brought into daily life.

非雨是面向开放创作的 IP 商业化发行平台。我们以消费品为出版媒介，连接 IP 方、创作者、生产方与消费者，支持 IP 的开发、出版与商业化。 RealRain is an IP commercialisation platform built for open creation. We publish through consumer goods, connecting rights holders, creators, manufacturers and consumers, and support the development, publishing and commercialisation of IP.

  1. 01

    交汇地Crossroads

    非雨运营的线上平台。IP 方在这里建立和经营 IP，创作者在授权范围内开发内容与周边，商品在平台市集发行。1.0 于 2026 年 9 月 30 日发布。 The online platform RealRain runs. Rights holders build and run their IP there, creators develop content and merchandise under licence, and goods are released in the platform’s market. Version 1.0 launched on 30 September 2026.

    进入交汇地Open Crossroads ↗
  2. 02

    消费品，是新的出版媒介Consumer goods, the new publishing medium

    传统出版把作品印成书。非雨出版 IP，用的是消费品：角色与作品在授权范围内被开发成周边和品牌商品，经过生产与发行，到达消费者手里。 A publisher prints a work as a book. RealRain publishes IP as products: within the scope of a licence, characters and works are developed into merchandise and branded goods, then manufactured and released to consumers.

  3. 03

    五项业务Five ways to work together

    原创 IP 发行、标准品周边托管、授权共创、品牌授权、生产合作。具体授权与履约范围，根据项目约定。 Original IP, merchandise hosting, licensed co-creation, brand licensing and production. Licensing and fulfilment scope are agreed project by project.
